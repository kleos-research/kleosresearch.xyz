Privacy

We collect nothing on this website.

This is a static site. There are no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. This page describes the little that does happen, and what our products do differently.

Last updated 4 August 2026

What this site does not do

kleosresearch.xyz is a set of static HTML pages. It does not set cookies, does not use local storage, contains no forms and no login, and runs no analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or session-recording scripts. We do not build profiles of visitors, and we have no visitor data to sell, share or lose.

What does happen

Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes the network requests needed to deliver pages and may log IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. That processing is governed by GitHub's privacy statement, not by us. We do not receive those logs and have no access to them.

Fonts. Pages load the Newsreader, Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes a request to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which discloses your IP address and user-agent to Google. We receive nothing from this, but it is a third-party request and you should know it occurs. We intend to self-host these fonts so that the site makes no third-party requests at all.

Links out. Pages link to GitHub and our own subdomains. Following a link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Email

If you write to contact@kleosresearch.xyz, we receive your message, your address and anything else you choose to include. Mail is handled by Google Workspace. We keep correspondence only as long as it is useful to answer you or to maintain a record of a discussion, and we do not add correspondents to any mailing list.

Our products are separate — and local by design

This policy covers the website. Our products have their own, stronger position, because privacy is an architectural decision for us rather than a legal one:

  • 0xCopilot is a local-first desktop agent. It runs on your machine and uses your own provider key. We do not route your prompts, files or credentials through our servers.
  • Kaleidoscope is a local memory system. The evidence store stays on your machine. It is the most sensitive material in the system and it is not designed to leave your disk.

Where a product connects to a third party at your instruction — a model provider, or a tool you connect — data goes to that party under its terms, and the product is built to make that visible rather than implicit.

Your rights

Because we hold no visitor data, most access and deletion requests have nothing to act on. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to contact@kleosresearch.xyz and we will delete it. If you are in a jurisdiction granting you statutory data rights, you may exercise them at the same address.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we knowingly collect no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Because the site collects nothing, we do not expect material change unless we add a feature that does — and we would say so plainly here before shipping it.

Plain summary. No cookies. No analytics. No accounts. No data collected. Your browser fetches fonts from Google, and our host logs requests for security. That is the whole story.
